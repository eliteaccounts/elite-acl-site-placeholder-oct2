2 · The fear, said out loud

Your knee has a plan. Your identity doesn't.

To the athlete

An ACL injury takes more than the season. It takes the pride of working hard, the people you train with, the thing you did every day without thinking about it.
So you start asking the question you won't say out loud.
Will I get back to who I was?

To the parent

You're watching your kid go quiet. You read about it late at night. You want to help, and you don't know what helping looks like.
So you start asking a question of your own.
Are we doing enough?

Both questions are fair. Neither one has to be answered alone.
